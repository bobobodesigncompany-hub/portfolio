Data Management Plan for SSNs and Health Information(Homework from Data Analysis)

As we approach 2050, the world will see a significant shift in demographics, with the 65+ population growing at an unprecedented rate. This project explores how we can prepare for a secure, fulfilling, and peaceful old age in the face of these changes.

1. Social Security Numbers (SSNs)

Data TypePersonally Identifiable Information (PII)
Field SpecificationsString, format "XXX-XX-XXXX"; Unique, non-null
Who Can ViewData Analysts, IT Team, Supervisors, Medical Staff
Who Can EditData Analysts, IT (authorized)
Who Can DeleteData Governor Only
Metadata StandardsHIPAA-compliant, encryption, access logs, version control
Data QualityValidation, manual spot checks, cleaning flagged records

2. Health Information (PHI)

Data TypeProtected Health Information (PHI)
Field SpecificationsMedical records, diagnoses, treatments, prescriptions
Who Can ViewDoctors, Nurses, Medical Staff, Analysts (anonymized)
Who Can EditDoctors, Nurses, Medical Staff
Who Can DeleteData Governor Only
Metadata StandardsAccess logs, version control, retention rules
Data QualityCorrections logged, errors flagged, anonymization enforced

3. Demographic Information

Data TypeGeneral Personal Information
Field SpecificationsName, Address, DOB "YYYY-MM-DD", Gender
Who Can ViewAnalysts, Medical Staff, HR
Who Can EditAnalysts, Medical Staff, HR
Who Can DeleteData Governor Only
Metadata StandardsAccess logs, source tracking, retention rules
Data QualityRegular updates, completeness, correction logging

4. Insurance Information

Data TypeFinancial and Billing Info
Field SpecificationsPolicy number, provider, exp date "YYYY-MM-DD", coverage
Who Can ViewBilling Dept, Medical Staff, Analysts (anonymized)
Who Can EditBilling Dept
Who Can DeleteData Governor Only
Metadata StandardsAccess logs, policy history, retention rules
Data QualityValidation, reviews by Data Governor

5. Contact Information

Data TypePersonal Contact Information
Field SpecificationsEmail "user@example.com", Phone "+1-XXX-XXX-XXXX"
Who Can ViewAnalysts, Medical Staff, HR
Who Can EditAnalysts, Medical Staff, HR
Who Can DeleteData Governor Only
Metadata StandardsAccess logs, update tracking, retention period
Data QualityRegular updates, deletion review, log review

6. Employment Information

Data TypeEmployee Information
Field SpecificationsSalary "$X,XXX.XX", Position, Employment Dates
Who Can ViewHR, Analysts (anonymized)
Who Can EditHR
Who Can DeleteData Governor Only
Metadata StandardsAccess logs, update tracking, legal retention rules
Data QualityHR reviews, audits, legal compliance
