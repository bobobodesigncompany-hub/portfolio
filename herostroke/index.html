What makes it different

Cantonese and Mandarin Your child picks the voice for every spoken question, and can switch Traditional or Simplified characters.
Real stroke practice Not just flashcards. Kids trace characters, then prove they can pick them out by ear.
Speak to check The app listens and tells them if they read it right. We do not keep recordings.
Collect the map Beat a district boss, earn a certificate, and unlock the next city.
