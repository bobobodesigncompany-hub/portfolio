Delete your Hero Stroke account

Hero Stroke — published by BoBo Design
You can delete your account yourself from inside the app, or ask us to do it for you. Either way the account and the data listed below are removed permanently — this cannot be undone.

Option 1 — delete it in the app

  1. Open Hero Stroke and sign in, if you are not already.
  2. Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner of the map screen.
  3. Scroll down to Account.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you signed in with an email address, you will be asked for your password once more to confirm it is really you. If you signed in with Google or Apple, you may be asked to sign in again for the same reason.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it

Email bobobodesign.com@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We will delete it and reply to confirm, normally within a few days and always within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your account (name, email address, account identifier) Deleted immediately, and removed from backups within 30 days.
Your children's names, birthdays, goals and learning progress Deleted. This is stored on your device and is removed when you delete the account or uninstall the app.
Subscription and payment records Kept by the app store, not by us. Google Play and Apple keep their own record of purchases for tax and accounting reasons. We cannot delete those; see their privacy policies. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it in Google Play > Subscriptions or in your Apple ID settings.

Playing without an account

Hero Stroke can be used as a guest, with no account at all. In guest mode nothing is sent to us: progress stays on the device and disappears when the app is uninstalled.

See also our Privacy Policy and Support page.
